## The sceptical student

I would use it this week for one list of up to twelve things, and this time I would not lose my work: I refreshed three times on purpose and it took me back to the same place each time. Typing "vaso" for *el vaso* and "sarten" without the accent both counted, and when it marked me wrong it let me overrule it. I still would not put my real course on it: my lecture notes came back as cards called "Again releasing carbon dioxide" with a pencil as the picture, my thirty elements became twelve with advice to "build the next one on the same route" (the one thing its own pitch says not to do), and my Spanish words all got the same picture-of-the-meaning "whispering" the word, which is a flashcard with extra steps. I have not seen the AI it is named after do anything.

## What is good and must not be changed

- **Resume.** "You were on a walk: ..., recall, place 4 of 10. Resume your walk / Start over" is exactly right, and recording each answer as it is given is what makes it safe.
- **The recall moment.** Pigment on the object, a wash on the wall, an ink ring on the floor. It is the product's idea in one frame.
- **Typed grading and its stated rules**, with "You wrote: ..." and "I was right".
- **The candour of the comparison result screen** ("Read these numbers as an anecdote ... each arm uses a different list ... It is not a study of Memoria's efficacy") and of `docs/venue-test.md`, including "report it exactly as it is, including if the palace did not win".
- **The results text** in the app: both meta-analyses, the bias warning, same-session recall, no efficacy study. It is more accurate than the pitch script.
- **The read gate and the offline labels** on the proposals screen, and "Best for ordered or discrete facts. It will not teach understanding." on the paste screen.
- **The hand-written carpal images and their "strengthen" alternatives.**
- **The pitch interstitial that waits**, the pinned actions on the results panel, the focus ring, and the title screen that now shows the palace.
- **README's "What we do not claim" section.**

## Not tested, and why

- **Live Palace Architect**: no key, no deployed URL. Everything above about image quality concerns the offline planner and the fixtures.
- **Motion**: glides, the recall flourish and the results tour were seen only as stills; frame rate was not judged.
- **`scripts/smoke.mjs` and `scripts/ab-smoke.mjs`**: not run, for time.
- **Whether a person can encode at 4 s per place**: still an inference from word counts.
- **The palace arm's score in the comparison test**: my script answered that arm blindly, so I checked only that the table records and averages what was entered. A second participant, "Next participant", "Copy as CSV" and "Clear all results" were not exercised, nor `?compare=list-first` and `?compare=palace-first`.
- **Strengthened alternatives** for seven of the eight bones; "Use this image" was not pressed, so I did not confirm the replacement is saved or shown on the next walk.
- **Route-reuse warning**, "This image is mine", moving an item between places (README says it is not built), deleting a palace.
- **Theme Lab** with a real pointer on sliders and colour fields (fields were set from script), and whether its buttons can be reached by Tab.
- **Two tabs**, and browser Back anywhere except during a walk.
- **`?perf`, `?debug`, the cyanotype and nocturne themes.**
- **Screen readers**: roles, names and live regions were read from the DOM; nothing was listened to. Contrast was not re-measured; review 1 found none under 4.5:1.
- **The loading veil in the default theme**: the text "The palace is being drawn" was seen in the DOM for the first two frames on greybox; my default-theme capture came too late to show it.
