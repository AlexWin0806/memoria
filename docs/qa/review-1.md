# QA review 1: fresh-eyes judge and sceptical student

Reviewer: QA / Judge agent, no knowledge of the build. 14 November 2026, 09:33 to 10:05 UTC.
Build under test: `npm run build` of the working tree, served with `vite preview`, driven with
headless Chromium (Playwright, software WebGL). Behaviour was tested on `?theme=greybox`; looks
were judged from seven screenshots of the default theme. `npm run check` passes. Scripts, logs
and screenshots are in the session scratchpad under `qa1/`, not in the repo.

Method note: every timing below was measured on greybox, where the frame rate is not the
bottleneck, and then confirmed against `src/flows/timing.ts`.

## The sceptical student

I would try it once, for a list of twelve or fewer things in a fixed order, and I would enjoy the walk. I would not rely on it next week: without the live Architect the images are things like "an elephant races around the lampstand" for "Element 1 - Hydrogen" (three elephants in twelve places), so I would be writing every image myself in a narrow side panel, and one refresh loses all of that. My anatomy list has thirty items and the app keeps twelve and tells me to "put the rest in another palace later" when there is only one palace, which it then warns me not to reuse. And when I type "a glass" for *vaso* it marks me wrong with no way to say I was right, so I would switch back to Anki for anything I am graded on.

## What is good and should not be changed

- **The honesty of the results text.** "Two meta-analyses ... both warn of a high risk of bias", "This score is same-session recall", "We have not run an efficacy study of Memoria", and "four of the eight ... the other four were not tested" all match research.md. Do not shorten these for space.
- **The offline fallback's candour.** "This is an offline draft ... plain placeholders", "Weak link: mercury and melon share only their first two letters. Rewrite this one." It fails gracefully in 0.4 s when `/api/architect` is missing. Keep the labels even when the planner improves.
- **Recall waits for the learner.** The question never times out; only the feedback auto-advances. Number keys 1 to 4, Enter and Space all work; focus moves to "Next place" after an answer.
- **Route walk and route check in the real flow.** "Which place comes right after The Cloak Bench?" with "Not that one. Picture the walk and try again." is exactly principle 3. The fifth place announces its golden hand.
- **Focus ring** (2 px, accent colour, 3 px offset) on every control, and **`aria-live`** on each step and `role="alert"` on validation.
- **Contrast.** Computed from the live DOM in ink-vellum and ink-sanguine: no text under 4.5:1 and no text under 16 px on the title and encode screens (muted text on the panel is about 5.6:1).
- **Resilience** to bad storage, offline, tiny windows and key-mashing, as listed above.
- **The fixture images for the carpal bones.** They are tied to the meaning (ham on a hook for hamate, android for trapezoid) and are the best content in the app.
- **The "Leave this walk? It will not be recorded." dialog**, with focus on "Stay".

## Not tested, and why

- **Live Palace Architect** (no API key, no deployed URL): planning, self-critique, rewriting of weak images, strengthening after a miss. Everything said above about image quality is about the offline planner and the hand-written fixtures.
- **Real frame rate and load time**: software rendering only.
- **`scripts/smoke.mjs` and `scripts/architect-smoke.mjs`**: not run, to stay inside the time budget. `npm run check` was run and passes.
- **A full default Judge Mode run to its results screen by hand.** I watched the preview and the first places and drove it by key-mashing; I did not read its results copy. The pitch variant was run to its results screen three times (mouse, keyboard only, default theme) and to the first question three more.
- **A review session on the due date.** After a recall the title shows "next review in 10 minutes" and Reviews shows "Nothing is due right now"; I did not advance the clock to see a due review run, and did not confirm by reload that the schedule itself persists (only that decks and settings do).
- **Typed recall on a learner-built deck, and whether "glass" alone is accepted for "glass (for drinking)".** My scripts for that flow stalled on their own bugs; typed grading was tested on the built-in Spanish deck only.
- **Two tabs writing at once** (whether one tab's save overwrites a palace created in the other). Only stale display was checked.
- **Looks of the non-default themes** (sanguine, nocturne, cyanotype), Theme Lab, `?perf`: not opened. Default-theme looks were judged from eight screenshots, none of them of the study or the route check, and one of the seven (feedback after a wrong answer) was captured but not examined.
- **Screen readers**: roles, names and live regions were inspected in the DOM; nothing was listened to.
- **Whether a human can in fact read and encode at 3.5 s per place** (issue 2): this is an inference from word counts and reading speed, not a measurement with people. It is the first thing to try on a teammate who has not seen the demo.
